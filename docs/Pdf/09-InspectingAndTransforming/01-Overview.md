---
title: Inspecting and transforming
description: Check what the writer produces, and inspect and transform existing documents.
---

These tools work on a [PdfDocument](cref:Meziantou.Framework.Pdf.PdfDocument) you created or opened. They report what they find as typed results, so that an
application can decide what to do with them.

## In this section

- [Round-trip validation](02-RoundTripValidation.md): check that a document is written the same way every time, and
  that writing it keeps its pages.
- [Optimizing documents](03-Optimizing.md): plan and apply the changes that make a document smaller or remove what
  it does not need, and repair harmless issues of a file.
- [Comparing documents](04-ComparingDocuments.md): compare what the pages of two documents show, by kind of change.
