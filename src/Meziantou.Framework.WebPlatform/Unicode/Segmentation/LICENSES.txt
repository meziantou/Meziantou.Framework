The word lists of this folder (*.dict) and the model of the Japanese phrase breaking (JapanesePhraseBreaker.Model.g.cs)
are compiled by tools/Meziantou.Framework.WebPlatform.SpecGen (SegmentationGenerator) from the data of ICU (https://github.com/unicode-org/icu):
icu4c/source/data/brkitr/dictionaries/*dict.txt and icu4c/source/data/brkitr/adaboost/jaml.txt. The data has been
modified: the word lists are converted into a binary trie, the model into tables. The code of SoutheastAsianWordBreaker
and JapanesePhraseBreaker is a port of the dictionary break engines of ICU (icu4c/source/common/dictbe.cpp, mlbe.cpp).
